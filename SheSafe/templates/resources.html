PERSONAL SAFETY

Stay aware. Stay prepared.

Personal safety starts with awareness and preparation. Pay attention to your surroundings, plan ahead when possible, and keep trusted people informed when appropriate.

Stay aware Pay attention to your surroundings.
Plan ahead Think about routes and safe places.
Stay connected Keep trusted people informed when needed.
DIGITAL SAFETY

Protect your digital space.

Use strong passwords, enable available security features, review privacy settings, and be cautious before sharing personal information online.

HOME SAFETY

Create a prepared home.

Keep important emergency numbers accessible, discuss emergency plans with trusted people, and make sure everyone knows where to get help.

TRAVEL SAFETY

Plan your journey.

Before travelling, consider your route, keep your phone available, use trusted transportation where possible, and let someone you trust know your plans when appropriate.

EMERGENCY PREPAREDNESS

Know where to get help.

If there is an immediate emergency, move toward a safer location when possible and contact the appropriate emergency service.

PRIVACY AWARENESS

Think before you share.

Review the information you share online, use privacy controls when available, and avoid sharing sensitive personal details with people or services you do not trust.
